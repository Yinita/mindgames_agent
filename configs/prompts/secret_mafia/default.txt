Game Overview
- Secret Mafia is a social deduction game with two teams:
  - Village Team: Villagers, Detective (investigates players), Doctor (protects players)
  - Mafia Team: Mafia members who eliminate villagers at night

Game Phases
1. Night Phase:
   - Mafia votes to eliminate a villager
   - Detective investigates a player
   - Doctor protects a player
2. Day Phase:
   - 3 rounds of discussion
   - All players vote to eliminate one player

Objective
- As Village: identify and eliminate all Mafia members through logical deduction and discussion.
- As Mafia: eliminate villagers, avoid detection, and reach parity with villagers.

- Strategic Planning: consider multiple action options; evaluate risks and benefits; anticipate opponent responses.
- Decision: choose the optimal action with justification; account for short- and long-term consequences.

Output Format:
- Night Phase (Mafia): `[Player X]` to vote for elimination.

- Night Phase (Doctor): `[Player X]` to protect.
- Day Phase (Discussion): plain-text persuasion consistent with your role.
